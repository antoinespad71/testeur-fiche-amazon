Product description or A+ content: which one to choose on Amazon?

By Antoine Spadaccini · updated on October 21, 2026

In short

The product description accepts 2,000 characters of plain text, without HTML. A+ content (image and text modules) is reserved for brands enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry; once published, it is shown instead of the description. Keep the description filled in anyway: the field stays saved, and specialists consider that it still counts for search, while Amazon has never confirmed that A+ text does.

Description and A+: the differences

Writing a good description

Plain text

No HTML tags: Amazon now only accepts plain text in this field. Break it up with line breaks and short sentences.

What the bullets didn't say

The bullet points give the main arguments. The description can tell how the product is used, detail its composition, care instructions or what's in the box.

Extra keywords

Its 2,000 characters let you place terms missing from the title, Item Highlights and bullets, while staying readable.

A+ content

Who can create it

You need a professional seller account and a role on a brand enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry (brand owner or brand representative, for example).

Basic A+ and Premium A+

Basic A+ offers standard image, text and comparison modules. Premium A+ adds larger visuals, videos and interactive modules; according to AMALYTIX, it requires among other things a published Brand Story and several approved A+ contents over the last 12 months.

What gets rejected

  • Prices, promotions and shipping information;
  • contact details, external links and QR codes;
  • unproven superlatives and unsupported environmental or health claims;
  • comparisons with competitors: comparison tables only show products of your own brand;
  • blurry images, images that are too small or have unreadable text.

Image alt text

According to ZonGuru, Amazon is gradually removing the alt text field from A+ modules and replacing it with automatically generated descriptions; in Europe, this removal is said to be in effect in 2026. So don't count on it to place keywords.

Which one to choose

  • You don't have access to A+: take care of the description, it is your only space for a detailed presentation;
  • you have access to A+: publish it to convince with visuals, and keep the description filled in for search.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters for an Amazon description?

2,000 characters maximum, in plain text, without HTML tags.

Does A+ content replace the description?

On screen, yes: when A+ content is published, it appears instead of the description. The description field stays saved in the listing.

Is A+ content text indexed by Amazon?

Amazon has never confirmed it, and specialists' tests suggest it does not improve keyword rankings. Put your keywords in the title, Item Highlights, bullets, description and search terms.

Who can create A+ content?

Professional sellers who have a role on a brand enrolled in Amazon Brand Registry.

Should I keep the description if I have A+ content?

Yes. It is no longer shown, but the field stays saved, and specialists consider that it is still taken into account by search.
