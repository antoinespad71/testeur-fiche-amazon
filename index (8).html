Amazon bullet points: the rules and a method for bullets that convert

By Antoine Spadaccini · updated on October 21, 2026

In short

A seller has 5 bullet points on Amazon. Since August 15, 2024, Amazon requires at least 3 bullets, each more than 10 and fewer than 255 characters long, starting with a capital letter, with no ending punctuation. Banned: emojis, symbols such as ™ or €, ASINs, "N/A" entries, money-back guarantees, contact details and links, and certain claims such as "eco-friendly" or "antibacterial". Each bullet should bring different information.

Amazon's rules for bullet points

Amazon tightened its requirements on August 15, 2024. A non-compliant bullet can trigger a warning in Seller Central, be changed automatically or be removed.

Number and length

  • 5 bullets for a seller, at least 3 required;
  • more than 10 and fewer than 255 characters per bullet;
  • one piece of information per bullet: no two bullets saying the same thing.

Formatting

  • A capital letter at the start of each bullet;
  • no ending punctuation: the bullet is written as a sentence fragment;
  • a colon to separate a heading from the detail, a semicolon to separate two ideas.

On top of these, the bans that apply to the whole listing: promotional or subjective terms (best, no. 1, sale…) and competitors' brand names.

Writing bullets that sell

One bullet, one benefit

Start with what the shopper gains, then give the feature that proves it. "Keeps water cool all day" says more than "double wall".

The decisive arguments first

Many shoppers only read the first two bullets. Put what sets you apart from your competitors there.

A short heading at the start

A few words as a heading, followed by a colon, help shoppers scan the listing at a glance. Amazon allows this separator between the heading and the detail.

Keywords placed naturally

Bullets are used by search. Place the terms that fit neither in the title nor in Item Highlights, without stacking them: the text has to stay readable.

Example

Fictional example, built for illustration. On Amazon.fr, bullets are written in French.

Before

✅ MEILLEURE QUALITÉ : gourde 100% écologique, satisfait ou remboursé !

Emoji, subjective term ("best quality"), "eco-friendly" claim, money-back guarantee and ending punctuation.

After

CHAUD OU FROID TOUTE LA JOURNÉE : la double paroi en acier inoxydable garde l'eau fraîche 24 heures et le thé chaud 12 heures FACILE À NETTOYER : le large goulot laisse passer une éponge et des glaçons ; le bouchon se démonte entièrement

Durations and composition must come from the product's technical sheet: don't write anything you can't prove.

Frequently asked questions

How many characters for an Amazon bullet point?

More than 10 and fewer than 255 characters per bullet, according to Amazon's requirements in force since August 15, 2024.

How many bullet points should I fill in?

Amazon requires at least 3, and a seller can fill in 5. Use all 5: each one is an extra argument and extra keywords.

Can I use emojis in bullet points?

No. Emojis and decorative symbols (✅, ™, €, ©…) are banned in bullets.

Should each bullet end with a period?

No. Amazon asks you to write each bullet as a sentence fragment, with a capital letter at the start and no ending punctuation.

Can I write "eco-friendly" in a bullet?

No. Amazon bans claims such as "eco-friendly", "environmentally friendly", "antibacterial" or "antimicrobial" in bullets.
