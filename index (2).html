      Frequently asked questions

      Amazon.fr listing rules, made simple

      Why did Amazon change my listing title?

      Since July 27, 2026, Amazon limits titles to 75 characters, spaces included, in every category except media products (books, music, video, software). For longer titles, Amazon's AI suggests a shortened version. Brand owners have 14 days before it is applied to review, edit or approve it, under "Review listing changes" in Seller Central.

      How many characters for an Amazon title?

      75 characters maximum, spaces included. Start with the brand, then the product and its main selling point. The same word should not appear more than twice, and characters such as ! $ ? _ { } ^ are not allowed, nor are promotional terms (best, sale, no. 1…).

      What is the "Item Highlights" field?

      On Amazon.fr it is called "Caractéristiques de l'article" (sometimes "Point fort de l'article"). It accepts 125 characters and appears under the title, in search results and on the listing. According to Amazon, search uses it just like the title. It holds what no longer fits in the title: material, age range, compatibility, use.

      Is the description still useful if I have A+ content?

      Yes. A+ content is shown instead of the description, but the field stays saved on the listing, and specialists consider it still counts for search, while Amazon has never confirmed that A+ text does. Its 2,000 characters let you add extra keywords without changing what shoppers see.

      What does this check cover, and what doesn't it cover?

      It checks how compliant and complete your listing is: title length and format, Item Highlights, bullet points, description and visuals. It cannot see your backend keywords, which are not public, and it does not measure keyword relevance or how you rank against competitors: that is what the full optimization is for.
