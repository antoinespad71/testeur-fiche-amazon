Optimizing an Amazon listing: the complete checklist

By Antoine Spadaccini · updated on October 21, 2026

In short

A complete Amazon.fr listing means: a title of 75 characters maximum with the brand, Item Highlights of 125 characters, 5 bullets of fewer than 255 characters, a description of 2,000 characters or A+ content, search terms under 249 bytes, at least 6 images in addition to the main image on a white background, and a video. This checklist covers every point to check, with a link to the detailed guide.

Title

  • 75 characters maximum, spaces included
  • The brand included, ideally at the start
  • The product and its main differentiator
  • No word more than twice
  • No banned character: ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦
  • No promotional or subjective term, no emoji

Detailed guide: Amazon changed my listing title.

Item Highlights

  • Field filled in, 125 characters maximum
  • No repetition of the title
  • Material, age range, compatibility or use
  • The most useful information first, as it is truncated on mobile

Detailed guide: Item Highlights.

Bullet points

  • 5 bullets filled in (3 minimum)
  • More than 10 and fewer than 255 characters each
  • A capital letter at the start, no ending punctuation
  • Different information in each bullet
  • No emoji, symbol, guarantee or contact details

Detailed guide: Amazon bullet points.

Description or A+ content

  • Description filled in, 2,000 characters maximum, no HTML
  • A+ content published if your brand is enrolled in Brand Registry
  • Description kept even with A+
  • No price, promotion, link or comparison with a competitor in the A+

Detailed guide: description or A+ content.

Search terms

  • Fewer than 250 bytes, accents counted double
  • No word already in the title or Item Highlights
  • No brand, no ASIN
  • Separated by spaces, no punctuation

Detailed guide: Amazon search terms.

Images and video

  • Main image on a pure white background, whole product, about 85% of the frame
  • No text, logo or watermark on the main image
  • At least 6 secondary images: in use, dimensions, details, arguments
  • At least 1,000 pixels on the longest side for zoom
  • A product video

Detailed guide: Amazon product images.

Offer

  • "Add to Basket" button present, in your name
  • Price consistent with the one charged elsewhere, including on your website
  • Stock available
  • Right category and right product type

Frequently asked questions

What are the character limits of an Amazon listing?

Title: 75 characters. Item Highlights: 125 characters. Bullets: fewer than 255 characters each. Description: 2,000 characters. Search terms: fewer than 250 bytes.

How many images should an Amazon listing have?

One main image on a white background, and at least six secondary images as recommended by Amazon, plus a video.
