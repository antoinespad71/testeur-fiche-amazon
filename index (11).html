Antoine Spadaccini, Amazon SEO specialist

I optimize Amazon.fr product listings for brands and sellers, in native French.

In short

My name is Antoine Spadaccini. After more than five years on the brand side managing listings on Amazon and Cdiscount, I now help sellers make their Amazon.fr listings more visible and more convincing. I rewrite the title, Item Highlights, bullets, description and search terms, based on an analysis of your competitors and a keyword research, and only from your own product information. Listings are written in native French, for French shoppers. Orders go through Fiverr, where my clients rate me 4.8/5 from 17 reviews.

My background

I spent more than five years on the brand side, managing product listings on Amazon and Cdiscount. That's where I learned what makes a listing get found, and what makes it sell: a clear title, arguments that speak to the shopper, visuals that answer their questions.

Today, I put this experience to work for sellers and brands whose Amazon listings are poorly optimized: hard to find in search results, or visited but not convincing enough to trigger a purchase. I rework each listing in full, for both search ranking and conversion, following Amazon's latest rules.

What I do

Full optimization of Amazon.fr listings

For each listing:

  • an analysis of 3 direct competitors: their titles, arguments, prices and weak points;
  • a research of 10 keywords, each placed in the right field;
  • a rewrite of the title (75 characters), Item Highlights (125 characters), the 5 bullets, the description (2,000 characters) and the search terms (249 bytes).

Audit of existing listings

With the 5-listing package and custom offers, I first audit your current listings: title compliance, product type, Buy Box, price, A+ content, bundles and stock. I also flag any issue I spot along the way, even outside the scope of the order.

Full catalogs

For a whole product range, I run a single competitor analysis for the entire range, and adapt bundles from the single-item listings.

My method

Nothing made up

I only work from your current listing, your website and your documents. No dimension, material, certification or claim that isn't in your sources. For food supplements, I stick to the claims authorized by EU regulation 1924/2006.

Amazon's rules, up to date

I follow Seller Central announcements and summarize them in free guides: the title limit, Item Highlights, bullets, search terms.

Counts checked

The length of each field is checked by a script, in characters and bytes, not by eye.

A ready-to-paste deliverable

You receive a clear PDF, field by field, to paste straight into Seller Central.

A few projects

  • A food supplement laboratory: 29 listings, bundles included, with a competitor analysis of the whole range and an audit of the existing listings;
  • a football accessories supplier: native French proofreading of translated listings and titles rebuilt at 75 characters;
  • a children's toy brand and a professional hygiene products supplier: listings optimized and delivered ready to paste.
